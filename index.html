About the Shuraim PPT

This Shuraim PPT is a Class 7 History presentation on Mobile and Settled Communities. It explains how medieval Indian society grew more complex, how tribal societies and nomads such as the Banjaras connected regions through trade, and how kingdoms like the Gonds and the Ahoms rose to power.

What is inside this PPT

Medieval society and caste groups

New occupations, wealth and political power created many groups within and beyond the older varna structure.

Tribal societies

Communities cultivated, herded, hunted and gathered, and barter linked them with settled societies. Stories, songs and ballads preserved their history.

Banjaras: trade and mobility

Pastoral nomads carried goods in large caravans, with temporary camps called tandas.

The Gonds and Rani Durgavati

Gond tribes united in the fifteenth century to form Garha Katanga. Rani Durgavati ruled for her son Bir Narayan and defended the kingdom.

The Ahoms of the Brahmaputra valley

The Ahoms migrated in the thirteenth century, worked with local landlords called bhuyans, and kept royal chronicles called buranjis.

Lachit Borphukan and the Battle of Saraighat

In 1671 Ahom forces defeated the Mughals in a naval battle at Saraighat. 24 November is celebrated as Lachit Divas.

The paik system

Paiks were men aged 15 to 60 who served as soldiers and builders. Four members formed a got, and service was rotated. Paiks were later organised into khels.
